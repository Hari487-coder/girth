Girth is an interactive hackathon prototype. All people, project records, employment opportunities, payments and certificates shown are fictional demonstration data. It does not accept money, offer real jobs or certify real training.

What this demo does

A sponsor can fund a simulated planting batch. A member can activate a ₹300 demo pass for 30 subscribed days, complete lessons and a quiz, accept an assignment and submit a report. Operations can approve and record payment, updating sponsor progress and member earnings.

State is saved only in this browser. Switching roles is a demonstration control, not authentication. You can reset the data from the sidebar. Live payments, multi-user accounts, real field evidence, accredited training, site permissions and carbon calculations are outside this prototype.

Business assumptions

₹600 per tree for 36 months is an unvalidated pilot target. ₹10 per subscribed day is proposed membership pricing; it does not guarantee work or income. Task rates and hours are sample values. The proposed Kandi site is illustrative and is not an approved IITH partnership.
